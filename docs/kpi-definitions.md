# Sales KPI definitions

Business definitions agreed for the `sales` project. They are implemented **once**, in the
Unity Catalog metric views under `projects/sales/src/metric_views/`. The dashboard and any
SQL or Genie user get the same numbers from there. `projects/sales/src/sales/kpis.py`
restates the formulas in Python. Unit tests pin them, and the job's quality checks compare
the metric views against them on every run.

> All data is synthetic and generated by the job. No real or production data is used.

## Base measures (gold tables, additive)

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Gross sales | Σ quantity × unit price of **sales** lines |
| Discounts | Σ discount amount of sales lines |
| Returns | Σ refunded net amount of **return** lines |
| **Net sales** | gross sales − discounts − returns (excluding VAT) |
| COGS | Σ quantity × unit cost of sold units − cost of returned units |
| Transactions | number of **sales** receipts (returns excluded) |
| Units sold | Σ quantity on sales receipts |
| Traffic | visitors counted by the store door counter |
| Selling area | store selling area in m², counted once per store-month |

## KPIs (metric view `sales_store_metrics`)

| KPI | Measure | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Net sales | `net_sales` | gross − discounts − returns |
| Same-store sales growth | `same_store_sales_growth` | Σ net sales of comparable stores ÷ Σ their net sales same days last year − 1 |
| Gross margin | `gross_margin` | net sales − COGS |
| Gross margin % | `gross_margin_pct` | gross margin ÷ net sales |
| Sales per m² | `sales_per_m2` | monthly net sales ÷ selling area (with several store-months selected, the average per store-month) |
| Traffic | `traffic` | Σ visitors |
| Conversion rate | `conversion_rate` | transactions ÷ traffic |
| ATV (average transaction value) | `atv` | net sales ÷ transactions |
| UPT (units per transaction) | `upt` | units sold ÷ transactions |
| ASP (average selling price) | `asp` | net sales ÷ units sold, so **ATV = UPT × ASP** |
| Return rate | `return_rate` | returns ÷ gross sales |
| Discount rate | `discount_rate` | discounts ÷ gross sales |

Gross margin and gross margin % are also available **by product category** in the
`sales_category_metrics` metric view.

Every ratio is a ratio of sums, computed at whatever level you query (store, region, month,
total). Ratios are never averages of per-store ratios.

## Comparable (same-store) rule

A store is **comparable** in a month once it has traded **12 full calendar months** before
that month.

| Store | Opened | First full month | Comparable from |
|---|---|---|---|
| S005 Lakeside Mall | 2025-09-15 | 2025-10 | 2026-10 |
| S006 Airport Outlet | 2026-03-01 | 2026-03 | 2027-03 |

SSSG includes only comparable stores that have prior-year data, on both sides of the ratio.

## Prior year and year-on-year

* `*_py` measures cover the **same store and the same calendar days one year earlier**
  (like-for-like). For the current, incomplete month, the comparison therefore uses the
  same days of last year's month.
* `*_yoy` is the relative change (`0.05` = +5 %). Used for amounts and counts: net sales,
  gross margin, sales per m², traffic, transactions, ATV, UPT, ASP.
* `*_yoy_pts` is the change in percentage points (`0.012` = +1.2 pts). Used for rates:
  gross margin %, conversion rate, return rate, discount rate.

## Changing a definition

1. Change the measure in the metric view YAML.
2. Change the matching function in `src/sales/kpis.py` and its unit test.
3. Update this page.

The quality checks fail the job if the metric view and the Python reference disagree.
